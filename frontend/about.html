Who We Are

Behind every great endeavor is a story, a purpose, and a dream. Ours is rooted in our founding history, propelled by our mission, and illuminated by our vision for what's possible. Together, these elements shape our identity and guide our journey, inspiring us to strive for excellence and push beyong boundaries.

Young4chicks was born out of a passion to support small-scale farmers and poultry enthusiasts. Our founder, Mutebi Phoebe, recognized the challenges faced by the industry and set out to create a platform that would connect people, provide valuable resources, and promote sustainable practices.

Revolutionizing the poultry industry by providing a seamless platform for buying, selling, and exchanging knowledge.

How Young4Chicks Works

Everything you need to know about buying and selling poultry in one simple platform.

Join Young4Chicks in minutes! Simply sign up with your email and start exploring our poultry marketplace. Whether you want to buy chicks for your backyard or sell birds from your farm, everything starts with a free account.

Once you're signed up, you can browse local sellers, check out different chicken breeds, and see current prices in your area. It's that simple to get started!

Looking for specific chicken breeds? Use our search filters to find exactly what you need. Filter by breed, age, price, and location to discover the perfect birds for your farm or backyard coop.

See photos of the chicks, read seller reviews, and compare prices before you buy. Contact sellers directly through the app to ask questions or arrange pickup times.

Got chickens, ducks, or other poultry to sell? Create your listing in just a few clicks! Add photos, set your price, and write a short description. Your listing goes live immediately for local buyers to see.

Manage all your sales from one dashboard. Track which birds are sold, chat with interested buyers, and get notifications when someone wants to buy from you.

All payments are secure and protected. Buy with confidence knowing your money is safe, and sell knowing you'll get paid quickly. We use trusted payment methods that work for everyone.

Both buyers and sellers are protected. If there's ever an issue, our support team is ready to help resolve any problems quickly and fairly.

New to raising chickens? No problem! We have guides on chicken care, feeding tips, and coop setup. Plus, our friendly community is always ready to share advice.

Need technical help with the app? Our support team responds fast to help you with any questions about buying, selling, or using Young4Chicks.
